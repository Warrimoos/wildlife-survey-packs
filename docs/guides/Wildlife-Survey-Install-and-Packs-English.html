Installing the app and adding packs

African Wildlife Survey on Android phones, without the Play Store

This guide shows how to install the app from a file (sideloading), and how to add the photo and sound packs. It takes about ten minutes. The pictures of phone messages are simple drawings, so your phone may look a little different. Phone messages are shown in English, as most phones display them.

Before you start

  • The app file: AfricanWildlifeSurvey.apk, about 33 MB.
  • The pack files you want (zip files, see step 8).
  • About 150 MB of free space on the phone.
  • Wifi if you are downloading. No internet is needed if a friend sends the files phone to phone.

1. Get the app file

  • From a link: open the download page link, tap Download the app, then tap Download if asked.
  • From a friend: ask them to send the APK by Quick Share, Nearby Share or Bluetooth. Tap Accept.
  • Either way the file is saved in your Downloads folder.

4. Allow installing from this source

  • The first time, the phone says it is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source.
  • Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then tap Back.
  • You can turn this off again afterwards.

5. Security checks

  • Play Protect may ask to scan the app. Tap Scan app and wait, or Install without scanning.
  • If it says the app is blocked, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  • Samsung phones: if Auto Blocker stops the install, go to Settings, Security and privacy, Auto Blocker, turn it off, install, then turn it back on.

6. Install and open

  • Tap Install and wait a few seconds.
  • Tap Open, or find Wildlife Survey with the kingfisher icon among your apps.

7. Allow location

  • When the phone asks to allow location, choose While using the app and keep Precise on.
  • This saves the exact spot of each record and works out your area and atlas square.
  • GPS works without mobile signal. Your location stays on your phone.

8. What packs are

  • The app comes with one photo of every species and no bird calls, to keep it small.
  • Packs add the rest: more photos for birds (32 MB), mammals (10 MB), insects (10 MB) and reptiles (3 MB), and bird calls (25 MB).
  • Add only the ones you want. Your records are never changed by packs.

9. Get the pack files

  • The easiest way is the Download button inside the app (step 10). To share without internet, download the zip files from the download page, or ask a friend to send them by Quick Share or Bluetooth.
  • Do not unzip them. If the phone offers to extract the file, say no.
  • They are saved in Downloads.

10. Add a pack in the app

  • Open the app and tap the Records tab.
  • Scroll to Photo and sound packs.
  • Tap Download beside a pack (best on wifi), or tap Add a pack from a file and choose a zip file in Downloads.
  • Wait for the message Pack added. The pack then shows Installed.
  • Repeat for each pack. You can delete the zip files from Downloads afterwards.

11. Pass it on without internet

  • Keep the APK and the zip files on one phone.
  • In Files, press and hold a file, tap Share, then Quick Share or Bluetooth, and choose your friend's phone.
  • Your friend follows this guide from step 3.

12. Updates and problems

  • Updating: first make a Backup file (Records, Settings and backup). Then install the new APK over the top. Do not uninstall first, or your records are deleted.
  • If you installed from Google Play, update from Google Play, not from a file.
  • "Problem parsing the package": the file is incomplete. Download or receive it again.
  • "App not installed": free up space and try again.
  • "Pack not added": check you chose the zip file and did not unzip it.
