Warrimoo Wildlife: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Warrimoo Wildlife is a free app for keeping your own record of the Australian wildlife you see and hear. It is built and published by Steve Rose (Warrimoos).

Summary

The app does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your own devices unless you choose to share it.

Information stored on your phone

  • Your records: the species you log as seen or heard, with the date, time, region, count and any notes you add.
  • Location: if you allow location access and leave GPS tagging on, the app saves the GPS position of each record and uses your position to choose your current region. Location is used only while the app is open on screen. It is never collected in the background.
  • Settings: your chosen states, home patch and display choices.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Watch app

If you install the companion watch app, your species lists and the records you make on the watch pass directly between your watch and your phone over the link between them. They are not sent to us or to any server of ours. The watch app uses location only to tag a record while you are making it.

Sharing is your choice

The app can create a backup file, a spreadsheet (CSV) and a map file (KML). These are only created when you tap an export button, and they go only where you send them through your phone's share menu.

Internet use

The app works without an internet connection. When you choose to download photos, maps or calls for a state, the app fetches them from GitHub (github.com), where the files are hosted. As with any download, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic request details, under GitHub's own privacy statement. Nothing about your records is sent. If you zoom the map beyond the downloaded detail while online, extra satellite map images may be loaded from EOX IT Services (tiles.maps.eox.at). Opening a location in Google Maps, or a link to iNaturalist, the Atlas of Living Australia or a source page, opens that site or app under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): to tag records and choose your region. Optional. You can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Internet: only for the downloads and map images described above.
  • Vibrate: a short buzz when you log a record.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

You can remove any record in the app. To remove everything, tap Delete everything on the More screen, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: privacy@warrimoo.net. Other questions about the app: apps@warrimoo.net.
