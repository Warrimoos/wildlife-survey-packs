African Wildlife Survey: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

African Wildlife Survey is a free field guide, survey and education app for the wildlife of Botswana. It is published by Animal Doctors and built by Steve Rose (Warrimoos).

Summary

The app does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

Information stored on your phone

  • Your records: the species you log, with the date, time, area, count, notes and survey details you enter.
  • Location: if you allow location access and leave GPS tagging on, the app saves the GPS position of each record and uses your position to choose the current area and atlas square. Location is used only while the app is open on screen. It is never collected in the background.
  • Names: any observer names you type into a survey, and your own name if you add it in settings.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone), your records and settings are included in it like other app data, so they come back when you move to a new phone. Downloaded photo, map and sound files are left out because they can be downloaded again. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Sharing is your choice

The app can create files such as a backup, a spreadsheet (CSV), a map file (KML), a survey report or a picture card. These are only created when you tap an export or share button, and they go only where you send them through your phone's share menu. To help protect wildlife, the exact locations of sensitive species are rounded in exported files unless you turn this off.

Internet use

The app works without an internet connection. If you choose to download an optional photo or sound pack, the app fetches it from GitHub (github.com), where the packs are hosted. As with any download, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic request details, under GitHub's own privacy statement. Nothing about your records is sent. If you zoom the map beyond the built in detail while online, extra satellite map images may be loaded from EOX IT Services (tiles.maps.eox.at). Opening a location in Google Maps hands that location to the Google Maps app or website, under Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): to tag records and choose the area. Optional. You can turn it off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Vibrate: a short buzz when you log a record.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is used in schools and clubs. It does not collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

Use "Clear all sightings" in the app's settings, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything the app has stored on your phone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: privacy@warrimoo.net. Other questions about the app: apps@warrimoo.net.
