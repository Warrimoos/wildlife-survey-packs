# African Wildlife Survey: packs

Optional photo and sound packs for the African Wildlife Survey Android app, a field guide and survey tool for the wildlife of Botswana and the Okavango, southern Africa.

The app offers these on first launch (Get everything, or choose), and any time under Records, Photo and sound packs. You can also download a zip from the packs folder and add it in the app with Add a pack from a file. Do not unzip the files: the app opens the zip itself.

| File | Contents | Size |
|---|---|---|
| packs/pack-photos-bird.zip | More bird photos (the app has one of each bird) | 32 MB |
| packs/pack-photos-mammal.zip | All mammal photos | 14 MB |
| packs/pack-photos-insect.zip | All insect photos | 16 MB |
| packs/pack-photos-reptile.zip | All reptile photos | 4 MB |
| packs/pack-calls.zip | Bird calls | 25 MB |

Packs for Warrimoo Wildlife, Steve's personal record of the wildlife he sees and hears across Australia. They live in `warrimoo-wildlife/au`, one set for each state and territory (act, nsw, nt, qld, sa, tas, vic, wa). In the app, go to More, States and downloads. Do not unzip the files.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| warrimoo-wildlife/au/st-<state>.zip | One photo of each species plus the offline satellite map for that state |
| warrimoo-wildlife/au/xp-<state>.zip (or -1 and -2) | Two more photos of each species, split in two when large |
| warrimoo-wildlife/au/ca-<state>.zip | Bird and frog calls |

The zips directly in `warrimoo-wildlife` are the older NSW only packs from version 10.

Same licences as above: photos by iNaturalist contributors and calls from xeno-canto.org, each under the licence shown with its credit in the app, free for non commercial use only.
