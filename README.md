# African Wildlife Survey: packs

Optional photo and sound packs for the African Wildlife Survey Android app, a field guide and survey tool for the wildlife of Botswana and the Okavango, southern Africa.

Download a pack from the packs folder in this repo, or add it inside the app under Records, Photo and sound packs. Do not unzip the files: the app opens the zip itself.

| File | Contents | Size |
|---|---|---|
| pack-photos-bird.zip | More bird photos | 32 MB |
| pack-photos-mammal.zip | More mammal photos | 10 MB |
| pack-photos-insect.zip | More insect photos | 10 MB |
| pack-photos-reptile.zip | More reptile photos | 3 MB |
| pack-calls.zip | Bird calls | 25 MB |

# Warrimoo Wildlife: packs

Optional packs for Warrimoo Wildlife, Steve's personal record of the wildlife he sees and hears in New South Wales. They live in the warrimoo-wildlife folder. In the app, go to More, Packs and tap Download, or save a zip to the phone and use Add a pack from a file. Do not unzip the files.

| File | Contents | Size |
|---|---|---|
| warrimoo-wildlife/photos-bird.zip | Two more photos of each bird | 50 MB |
| warrimoo-wildlife/photos-mammal.zip | Two more photos of each mammal | 15 MB |
| warrimoo-wildlife/photos-reptile.zip | Two more photos of each reptile | 26 MB |
| warrimoo-wildlife/photos-frog.zip | Two more photos of each frog | 8 MB |
| warrimoo-wildlife/photos-invert.zip | Two more photos of each insect and spider | 35 MB |
| warrimoo-wildlife/calls.zip | 503 bird and frog calls | 43 MB |

Same licences as above: photos by iNaturalist contributors and calls from xeno-canto.org, each under the licence shown with its credit in the app, free for non commercial use only.
